Word Counter Online Free — Count Words, Characters & Sentences (2026)

Whether you're a writer, student, or content creator, a word counter helps you track your writing metrics in real time. Count words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and estimate reading time — all in your browser, nothing uploaded.

Why Use a Word Counter?

  • Meet requirements: Essays, articles, and assignments often have strict word limits
  • SEO optimization: Blog posts perform better at certain word counts (1500-2500 words)
  • Social media: Twitter has 280 characters, meta descriptions should be 150-160 characters
  • Readability: Track reading time to match audience attention span
  • Keyword density: Avoid keyword stuffing in your content

How Browser-Based Word Counters Work

Word counters use simple text analysis algorithms that run entirely in your browser:

  1. Split text by whitespace to count words
  2. Count individual characters (with and without spaces)
  3. Detect sentence boundaries with punctuation marks (. ! ?)
  4. Identify paragraphs by double line breaks
  5. Calculate reading time at 200 words per minute (average adult reading speed)
  6. Analyze keyword frequency and density

Common Word Count Guidelines

  • SEO blog post: 1500-2500 words
  • Social media post: 50-200 words
  • Meta description: 150-160 characters
  • Product description: 200-500 words
  • Academic essay: 500-5000 words (varies)
  • Newsletter: 500-2000 words
  • Twitter/X post: 280 characters max
  • SMS message: 160 characters per segment

Reading Time Estimates

  • Average adult reading speed: 200-250 words per minute
  • 500 words ≈ 2-3 minutes reading time
  • 1000 words ≈ 4-5 minutes reading time
  • 2000 words ≈ 8-10 minutes reading time
  • 5000 words ≈ 20-25 minutes reading time

Tips for Better Writing

  • Write first, edit later: Focus on getting ideas down, then use the word counter during editing
  • Vary sentence length: Mix short and long sentences for better readability
  • Check keyword density: Target keywords should appear naturally, not forced
  • Short paragraphs: Online readers prefer 2-4 sentence paragraphs
  • Use active voice: More engaging and typically shorter than passive voice

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a word counter work?
Word counters split your text by whitespace to count words, count individual characters, detect sentences by punctuation, and calculate reading time based on average reading speed.

Is an online word counter private?
Browser-based word counters process everything locally on your device. Your text never leaves your computer — no upload, no server, no tracking.

What is a good reading time?
Average reading speed is 200-250 words per minute. A blog post of 1000 words takes about 4-5 minutes to read.

Can I count words in different languages?
Yes — word counters work with any language that uses spaces between words. Character count works for all languages including Chinese and Japanese.

Is there a character limit?
Free: up to 10,000 characters. PRO: unlimited text and export reports.

Do you store my text?
No. All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or transmitted. Your text is gone when you close the page.
